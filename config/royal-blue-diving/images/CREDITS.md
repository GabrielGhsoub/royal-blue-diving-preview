## What is VERIFIED, from their published price list
Every price in this config is theirs, read off the Prices page on 2026-08-01:
- Discover Scuba Diving 90 EUR, equipment included.
- A second dive the same day 45 EUR.
- Certified divers, 2 dives over 1 day 125 EUR, air and ballast and equipment.
- Elephant cave boat trip from Venizien port, 2 hours navigation, 2 dives, 145 EUR.
- PADI Open Water 490 EUR, PADI theory support not included.
- PADI e-learning 105 EUR, offered at that price only to their own students.
- Masks and snorkels 45 EUR from the beach, 65 EUR from the boat, so the
  "from the boat" option is priced at the 20 EUR difference.
- Photos and video during the dive 20 EUR.
- Meet at 8:45 at the diving school; briefing in English or French.
- Open Monday to Saturday 08:30 to 17:00, so Sundays are blocked.
- A family team, half Greek half French, based at Panormo Geropotamou, Rethymno.
